Why drill with DevDrill

  • Interview-style questions

    Algorithms, data structures and OOP questions of the kind that show up in interviews, each with a short explanation.

  • Progress you can measure

    Accuracy, streaks and rounds played are tracked on your dashboard, so you can see yourself improving.

  • Daily dev joke

    There are 10 types of people: those who understand binary and those who don't.

  • Survival mode

    You get three lives. How long can your streak last before the third wrong answer?

  • Review what you missed

    Every wrong answer is saved, so you can replay only the questions that tripped you up.

  • Study mode

    Prefer no pressure? Flip through question cards at your own pace and reveal the answers.

Practice concepts

Pick a track to start. Live questions come from the Open Trivia Database; the algorithm, data structure, OOP and fun-fact tracks use a built-in question bank.
